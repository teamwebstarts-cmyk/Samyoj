Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 1, 2026  |  Last Updated: September 8, 2026

At Samyoj ("Samyoj", "we", "us", or "our"), operated by Samyoj Technologies, accessible from https://app.samyoj.com, we are committed to protecting the privacy and confidentiality of our business customers and their end users.

Meta Platform & WhatsApp Cloud API Notice Samyoj provides business software that connects directly to the official Meta WhatsApp Business Cloud API under the Tech Provider partner model. We process customer business data strictly to deliver WhatsApp messaging, team inbox management, automations, and analytics. We do not sell, monetize, or harvest personal data.

1. Roles and Scope (Data Controller vs. Data Processor)

Under applicable data protection laws, including the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP Act) of India and the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR):

  • Samyoj as a Data Controller: We act as a Data Controller for our direct account holders' information (e.g., registration details, billing contact, login credentials, and platform usage analytics).
  • Samyoj as a Data Processor: When our business customers connect their WhatsApp Business Accounts (WABAs) and communicate with their end customers, the business customer acts as the Data Controller. Samyoj acts solely as a Data Processor processing message logs, phone numbers, and customer conversations on behalf of and according to the instructions of our business customer.

2. Information We Collect

To provide our services, we collect and process the following categories of information:

A. Account & Registration Data

  • Identity Information: Name, business email address, company name, and hashed credentials.
  • Billing Details: GST number, company billing address, and transaction identifiers (processed securely via our payment gateway Razorpay; we do not store full credit/debit card numbers).

B. WhatsApp Business Account & Integration Data

  • Meta Credentials & Tokens: Meta Business Manager Portfolio ID, WhatsApp Business Account (WABA) ID, WhatsApp Phone Number ID, display phone number, and OAuth access tokens.
  • Encryption at Rest: All Meta OAuth access tokens and API secrets are strictly encrypted in our database using industry-standard AES-256-GCM encryption.

C. Messaging & Customer Communications Data

  • Inbound & Outbound Messages: Message text, timestamps, media URLs/metadata, delivery status updates (sent, delivered, read, failed), and conversation identifiers.
  • Contact Lists & Consent: Customer phone numbers (in international E.164 format), opt-in consent status records, and tags managed within the customer's workspace.

D. Gmail send access

  • Send only: When a business user connects Gmail or Google Workspace, Samyoj requests gmail.send. We do not read, list, or change mailbox contents.
  • What we store: The mailbox address, encrypted access and refresh tokens, and for each send the subject, body, recipient, Gmail message id, and status, so the user can see the sent list.
  • Attachments: Files attached to a send stay private to that organization, are limited to allowed types, and expire after 24 hours.
  • Not for ads: We do not use this mailbox data for advertising, and we do not sell it.
  • Disconnect: Disconnecting the mailbox deletes the stored tokens.

E. Technical & Log Data

  • Internet Protocol (IP) addresses, browser type, device information, operating system, and system activity logs necessary to diagnose platform uptime and prevent fraudulent abuse.

3. How We Use Information

We process personal and business data for the following legitimate business purposes:

  • To enable two-way WhatsApp messaging via the Meta Cloud API for shared team inboxes and customer support.
  • To execute scheduled broadcast campaigns and automated message workflows configured by the customer.
  • To provide real-time notification updates to agents via secure WebSockets.
  • To deliver message delivery analytics, template status tracking, and quality rating insights.
  • To manage subscription billing, account security, two-factor authentication, and customer support.
  • To send email the business user wrote, from the Gmail or Google Workspace mailbox they connected, to contacts who already have email permission.
  • To comply with Meta Platform Policies, WhatsApp Business Policies, and legal compliance obligations.

4. Data Sharing & Third-Party Service Providers

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data. We share information only with authorized service providers under strict confidentiality agreements:

Provider Purpose Data Shared
Meta Platforms, Inc. WhatsApp Business Cloud API message delivery and webhook routing WABA ID, Phone Number ID, message payloads, destination phone numbers
Razorpay Software Pvt. Ltd. Subscription billing and GST invoice processing Organization name, billing email, billing address, plan SKU
Google Send email from the mailbox a business user connected Mailbox address, OAuth tokens, and the message the user chose to send
Cloud Hosting & Database Encrypted data storage and compute infrastructure Encrypted application records and system logs

5. Meta Tech Provider & Billing Model Transparency

In accordance with Meta's Price Transparency and Service Provider Terms:

  • Samyoj is a Tech Provider. Samyoj invoices the customer solely for software subscriptions, features, seats, and support.
  • Meta charges conversation and template delivery fees directly to the customer's own Meta Business Manager / WABA payment method. Samyoj does not hide Meta delivery charges or resell WhatsApp messaging credits at unauthorized markups.

6. Data Security & Retention

We implement rigorous technical and administrative safeguards:

  • Transport Layer Security: All data transmitted between clients, our servers, and Meta Graph API endpoints is protected using modern TLS 1.3 / HTTPS.
  • Token Security: All Meta system and business tokens are encrypted at rest using AES-256.
  • Tenant Isolation: Customer organizations and conversation records are logically isolated through strict database constraints.
  • Retention Period: Active account data is retained for the duration of the customer's subscription. Upon account closure, data is retained for up to 30 days to permit customer data export before permanent automated deletion.

7. Your Privacy Rights (DPDP & GDPR)

Depending on your jurisdiction, you and your end customers have the following rights:

  • Right to Access: You may request an export of your stored organization data.
  • Right to Rectification: You can update and correct your profile and business details at any time in Settings.
  • Right to Erasure (Data Deletion): You can request permanent deletion of your organization, phone number links, and message logs. Please refer to our Data Deletion Instructions.
  • Right to Revoke Consent: You may disconnect your WhatsApp number or revoke Samyoj's permissions from your Facebook Business Manager at any time.
