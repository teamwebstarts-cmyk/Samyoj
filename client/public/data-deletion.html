User Data Deletion Instructions

Effective Date: September 1, 2026  |  Last Updated: September 8, 2026

In accordance with Meta Platform Policy, the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP Act), and global data privacy standards, Samyoj provides clear, straightforward mechanisms for users, organizations, and end recipients to request the permanent deletion of their personal and business data.

Meta Platform Policy Compliance Notice Under Meta's Platform Policy, when you remove the Samyoj application from your Meta Business Manager or Facebook account, or if you request data erasure, Samyoj guarantees that all associated user data, access tokens, and stored communication metadata will be deleted in a safe and timely manner.

1. How to Request Data Deletion (Organization Owners)

If your business has created a workspace on Samyoj and you wish to delete your organization, connected WhatsApp numbers, or message logs, you can choose either of the following methods:

AMethod 1: Self-Service Deletion via Dashboard

  1. Sign in to your Samyoj account at https://app.samyoj.com/login.
  2. Navigate to Settings from the left sidebar.
  3. Go to the WhatsApp tab and click Disconnect Number to immediately revoke Meta token access and cease inbound/outbound messaging.
  4. Go to the Workspace / Account tab and select Delete Workspace Data.
  5. Confirm the deletion prompt with your account password.

BMethod 2: Email Request to Privacy Team

You may submit a formal data deletion request by emailing our privacy team:

  • Email: privacy@samyoj.com
  • Subject: Data Deletion Request - [Your Organization Name]
  • Required Information: Send the request from your registered account owner email address, specifying your Organization Name and registered WhatsApp phone number ID.

2. How End-Users / WhatsApp Recipients Can Request Deletion

If you are an individual recipient who received WhatsApp messages from a business using the Samyoj platform, you have the right to have your phone number and conversation records deleted:

  • Instant Opt-Out: Reply with "STOP" or "UNSUBSCRIBE" directly inside your WhatsApp chat thread. Our system automatically processes this keyword to immediately revoke marketing consent and prevent further automated broadcasts.
  • Permanent Erasure: To request complete erasure of your contact record and chat history from our database, email privacy@samyoj.com with your phone number in international format (e.g. +91XXXXXXXXXX). We will verify and purge all matching records across our platform.

3. Revoking App Access via Meta Business Suite

You can also revoke Samyoj's access directly through your Meta account:

  1. Log into Meta Business Suite or your Facebook account.
  2. Navigate to Settings → Business Integrations (or Apps and Websites).
  3. Locate Samyoj in the list of active apps.
  4. Click Remove.
  5. Meta will notify our servers, and our system will immediately deactivate your connected access tokens and pause all associated webhook integrations.

4. What Data Is Deleted and What Is Retained

Data Category Deletion Action Timeframe
Meta Access Tokens & Credentials Permanently deleted and revoked from database Immediately (Within 24 hours)
WhatsApp Message Records & Logs Permanently purged from database and cache Within 30 calendar days
Customer Contacts & Phone Numbers Permanently purged across all audience lists Within 30 calendar days
Tax Invoices & Financial Records Retained strictly for statutory compliance (GST/tax audits) Retained as required by law (7 years)

5. Confirmation and Tracking

Upon receipt of an email deletion request, our team will:

  1. Acknowledge receipt of your request within 48 hours and issue a unique Deletion Request Tracking ID.
  2. Complete the verification and purge process within 30 calendar days.
  3. Send a written electronic confirmation confirming that all applicable data has been permanently deleted from our primary and backup databases.
