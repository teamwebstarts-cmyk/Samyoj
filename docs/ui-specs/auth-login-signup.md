## Goal
Sign users into (or register for) the Samyoj workspace with a light SaaS marketing left panel and elevated form card on the right.

## Layout map
- Shell: full-bleed light off-white page with soft green ambient blobs
- Left (lg+): brand row, headline, subcopy, hero product image, trust strip
