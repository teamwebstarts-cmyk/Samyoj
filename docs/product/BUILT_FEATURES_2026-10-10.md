# Built features

In the app today. A row is here only if the screen or the send path exists in code.

Facebook, Instagram, and Ads still need Meta’s app approval before a real Page, comment, or ad account delivers on a customer host. The features below are built. That approval is not.

---

## WhatsApp

- Connect with Facebook login and pick the business number
- One workspace per connected number (inbox, contacts, and campaigns stay on that number)
- Disconnect keeps the number and the history
- Shared inbox
- Assign a chat, leave a note, search
- Ready replies
- Image, document, audio, and video in the thread
- Free-form reply inside the 24-hour window
- Approved template after that window
- Create a template, submit it to Meta, and see approval
- Starter template library (the business submits it on its own number)
- Buttons, lists, and a call-permission card
- If a chat came from an ad click, the thread can show that
- Keyword reply, welcome message, away message, business hours
- Visual flow: message, buttons, delay, handoff
- Send a test of a flow, and jump to a step from the inbox
- WhatsApp Forms inside a chat
- Call list and recents
- Post-call summary in the thread

Not built: handing a live call to a human agent.

---

## Instagram

- Connect the professional account that is linked to the Facebook Page
- DM inbox, separate from WhatsApp
- Reply inside the 24-hour window
- Comment list
- Hide or unhide a comment
- One private reply on a comment, within 7 days
- Keyword rule that runs on Instagram comments only
- After the person replies in DMs, the chat continues in Instagram Inbox
- Posts: image, Reel, or carousel
- Draft, schedule, publish now, cancel a schedule
- List of scheduled and published posts

Not built: Instagram-only login, a Stories editor, WhatsApp templates on Instagram.

---

## Facebook

- Connect a Facebook Page
- Messenger inbox, separate from WhatsApp
- Assign or close a chat
- Text reply inside the 24-hour window
- An Instant Form submission can be saved as a lead

Not built: creating Page posts, WhatsApp templates on Messenger.

---

## Ads

- Last 7 days from the connected ad account
- Amount spent, impressions, clicks, leads, cost per lead
- Label on the numbers: 7-day click / 1-day view
- Choose which ad account to read
- Open Ads Manager

Not built: create, edit, pause, or budget an ad inside Samyoj. No single “rupees earned from ads” number.

---

## Campaigns

WhatsApp only.

- Send an approved template
- Audience from contacts, tags, a typed list, or everyone who opted in
- Fill the template fields (name and the rest)
- Schedule a send
- Repeat a campaign
- Retry people who failed
- A follow-up if they do not reply
- Send does not go out unless the contact opted in, with a source and a time, and has not opted out
- Counts for sent, delivered, and read
- Pause, resume, reschedule, duplicate

Not built: the same campaign to Instagram, Messenger, or email.

---

## AI bot

- A chat bot and a calling bot are two separate bots
- About: personality and business details
- Train: answers and FAQs the bot can use
- Try it before it goes live
- Replies in the language the customer writes, including Hinglish
- Turn the bot off on one chat and reply yourself
- Turn a bot on or off for chats
- Calling bot: pick a voice and preview it
- The calling bot can answer a WhatsApp call
- Per contact: AI may answer, or a person only
- If calling AI is off for the business, no contact overrides that
- After the call, a summary is written in the chat

Not built: passing the live call to a human agent.
