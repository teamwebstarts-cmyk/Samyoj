## Motion — glass and moving type

The look of current product films. Chosen style: **Glassmorphism + Kinetic Typography**. One green **liquid wipe**. No neon city, glitch, VHS, chrome, or brutalism.

| | |
|---|---|
| Format | 16:9 website hero. 2:00. Master 4K, deliver 1080. |
| Camera | Slow dolly push on the story shots. Locked on the app, with a 3% push across the scene. Parallax only on the glass layers, 8px. |
| Timing | Titles in 12 frames, snappy. Inside the app, smooth. A beat only on the four dots and the last line. |
| Transitions | Night to day: liquid wipe. Every later scene: a glass panel slides 24px. No whip pan, no glitch cut. Those make real UI type look dirty. |
| Light | Night: soft rim light and four soft color blooms. Day: clean white, with that channel’s color at 8% in the corner. |
| Type | Plus Jakarta Sans 800. Entry: 24px slide plus scale 0.96→1. No bounce, no typewriter, no glitch reveal. |
| Under the voice | A whoosh on the wipe. A soft tick on each chip. One soft bass hit on the last line. Music sits 8–10 dB under the voice. |
| Reference | Buck and Giant Ant product films: big type, glass, one hero color. |
| Technical | Smooth, no jitter, no flicker, crisp edges, one design language. Not a loop. The start and the end are different. No watermark. |

Glass card (every app window): white at 92%, 1px edge `rgba(255,255,255,.7)`, 18px blur behind, shadow `0 40px 90px -30px rgba(15,23,42,.28)`, radius 24.
