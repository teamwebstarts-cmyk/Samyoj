## 4. Patterns to copy

- **14-day trial** (majority). We match.
- **Most Popular = middle tier** (Growth). We match.
- **Quarterly billing** in India. Default cadence quarterly.
- **Show Meta separately** (Respond.io / 360dialog / Twilio). We **must** (policy + trust).
- **Seat caps** if price is low (Gallabox). Our Starter 3 / Growth 10 is coherent.

## 5. Patterns to avoid

- Forever-free + surprise Meta prepaid wall (AiSensy-style confusion).
- 20–30% silent Meta markup inside SaaS (Wati/Freshchat pattern; Service Provider Price Transparency if we ever invoice Meta).
- Conversation-priced SaaS (Tidio) — wrong for our catalog (feature+quota).
- “Unlimited WhatsApp” in the headline.

## 6. Positioning sentence

**Samyoj sells the inbox operating system (seats, CRM, automation, AI). Meta sells the pipes (per-message + calling). Two bills, like Twilio+desk, unlike BSPs that wrap both.**
